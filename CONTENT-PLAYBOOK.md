# Content Playbook (plain)

Two lanes, one destination. Everything points back to Maya — local human
creativity deserves to thrive. (See THE-HEART.md.)

## The two lanes
- **Reach (the engine):** daily shorts + reactions/commentary on viral stuff.
  Fast, cheap, rides trends, builds your face and your audience.
- **Heart (the soul):** real local-maker stories, told beautifully. Slower,
  higher craft. Makes people *love* the mission and shows the product.

Reach brings the crowd. Heart turns the crowd into believers. Don't skip either.

## Rule #1 — the one that saves you
**Every reaction, every take, every stream threads back to your one obsession:
local, human, creativity, connection.** You are NOT a generic "smart take" guy
(that's the ego trap, and it's saturated). You're *the local/human lens on
everything.* Same worldview, applied to whatever's trending. That consistency is
what makes it a brand instead of noise.

## The reaction method (reach engine), on-brand
For any viral short:
1. **React** to it — ride the existing attention.
2. **Filter it through your lens** — "here's what this says about how we lost
   local / human / connection," or "here's what it could look like instead."
3. **Land on hope or agency** — not a doom rant, not a dunk.

Example: viral clip of a lonely city → "we built a world optimized for
convenience and lost each other — here's what local connection actually looks
like." Same lens, infinite material.

**Avoid:** generic political hot takes, leading with "sovereignty" or grand
theory, negativity-for-clicks. Heady and divisive shrinks the tent.

## The maker stories (heart = product = marketing)
- Go interview real local makers (Maya types). Film it.
- Each interview becomes **1 long-form** (the full story) + **3–5 shorts** (the
  emotional/striking moments).
- Triple-purpose: the interview *onboards* them, *markets* them, AND is your best
  content. One act, three jobs.
- These are the pieces that make people *feel* the mission — without you ever
  explaining it.

## Long-form / streaming
- Great for: depth, personality, **build-in-public** (document building this +
  helping makers), the maker sit-downs, "a day onboarding a local business."
- People root for someone building something real. Let them watch it happen.
- **Don't** turn the stream into a philosophy lecture. Talk *through* stories,
  makers, reactions, the build. The vision gets *felt*, not stated.

## Cadence (realistic — don't burn out month one)
- **Daily:** 1 short (a reaction OR a clip pulled from a maker story).
- **Weekly:** 1 long-form (maker interview / mini-doc / build-in-public stream).
- **Rare:** a deep "big picture / vision" piece — for the believers who lean in.
  Never the lead.

Daily *everything* is a grind, especially while you're stretched. Pick a rhythm
you can KEEP. Consistency beats heroics.

## Voice
Warm. Plain. Human. Felt, not preached. Hope and agency, not doom. You're the
person who sees the soul in local human creativity — and applies that lens to
the whole world.

## The throughline
Reactions build the crowd → maker stories convert them → both always point to:
*local human creativity deserves to thrive, and here's how we make it happen.*
The grand vision (sovereignty, the bigger ecosystem) is the **gravity
underneath** — discovered over time by the people who stay. Never the billboard.

## The one warning
Daily reaction/streaming makes *you* the brand. That's fine — IF you're the
storyteller *of the mission.* The danger is it drifting into "me being clever"
untethered from Maya. Rule #1 is the leash: if a piece doesn't ladder back to
local/human/connection, don't post it.
