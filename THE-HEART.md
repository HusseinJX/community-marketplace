# The Heart — who this is for

> Everything — WhatsLocal, the connector, the brand, even a trip — either serves
> her, or it doesn't get built.

## The one line
We exist so gifted local people can make a living from their gift — supported by
their community — **without having to become marketers.**

## Her — the person we serve (call her Maya)
Maya is a sound healer. Or a ceramicist, a muralist, a baker — someone genuinely
**good at a beautiful thing that people love.** But she's under-booked and
overwhelmed. She didn't get into this to fight algorithms, build funnels, or
hustle online. What she actually wants isn't "growth" — it's **clients,
community, events, collaboration.** To do more of the thing she's good at, and
less of everything else.

**If we aren't making Maya's life lighter and her calendar fuller, we're failing
— no matter how clever the product is.**

## Them — the shopper / neighbor
Someone who *wants* to support local — but only if it's easy. We give them two
things, and both must be true:
- **Convenience** — discover and back local creativity with near-zero effort.
- **Soul** — it *feels good.* Supporting a real human's gift beats buying from a
  faceless store. That feeling is the soul of "local."

## The whole thing, in your words
> More small local human creativity and the businesses behind it — and more
> money reaching them from shoppers — kept **convenient** for the shopper and
> **soul-fulfilling** for everyone.
>
> That's it. That's the soul of local.

## The loop
Maya does her craft → we connect her (discovery, collaboration, events) with
**~zero effort from her** → shoppers find and support her, conveniently and
meaningfully → she earns, books up, keeps creating → her thriving pulls in the
next Maya. **The web of thriving local makers *is* the resilient ecosystem.**

## The laws (every decision must pass all of these)
1. **Near-zero effort for Maya.** She does her gift; we do the connecting. If a
   feature adds to her overwhelm, it's wrong — even if it "works."
2. **Convenient for the shopper.** Friction kills support.
3. **Soul-fulfilling, not transactional.** Story, face, connection, meaning — not
   just a buy button.
4. **Connection over solo hustle.** Events and collaboration bring her clients —
   not another lonely night posting.
5. **Money must actually reach her.** If it doesn't help put food on Maya's
   table, it's decoration.

## The litmus test — ask this of everything
> **"Does this help Maya live from her gift — less effort, more connection — and
> make supporting her convenient and soul-fulfilling for the people around her?"**

- If **no** → don't build it.
- If **unsure** → you've drifted. Come back to Maya.

## Why this *is* the whole mission, not a small piece
Maya, multiplied by the thousands who exist in every town, **woven together so
they hold each other up** — that web of people thriving on their gifts,
supported by their community, *is* what resilience and emergent sovereignty look
like in the flesh.

We don't build down from the philosophy. **We build up from Maya.**
The mission is valid because she is real.
