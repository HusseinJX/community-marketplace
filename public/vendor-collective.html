§ 01 How it works

Four steps from hello to here's who you should meet.

WhatsLocal replaces the messy spreadsheet, the dead Facebook group, and the organizer who's always too busy to make the intro. Every member onboards through a conversation — and every conversation makes the network smarter.

  1. 1

    Members join by chatting.

    No forms. People sign up through a web chat or by texting a number. The concierge asks natural questions — what they make, what they're looking for, their vibe, their neighborhood — and builds a complete profile from the conversation.

    What do you make?
    Wheel-thrown stoneware. Mostly mugs, the occasional weird vase.
    Where are you based?
    Temescal, north Oakland.
  2. 2

    The agent enriches and understands.

    It automatically fills in the gaps — pulling public info from their site, their Instagram, their listings — and turns every profile into a deep searchable understanding, not just keywords but meaning.

    Maya Ortiz · joined 2 days ago
    ceramics stoneware temescal wholesale-curious vendor markets
    meaning → Solo maker, production-scale curious. Aesthetic leans muted & earthy. Wants wholesale relationships with cafés & restaurants. Open to pop-ups.
  3. 3

    It makes introductions.

    Once it knows enough, the concierge recommends the three best connections for each new member — a vendor who'd love their product, an organizer running their kind of event, a neighbor with shared interests — with a personal note on why.

    Maya Ortiz
    Ceramics · Temescal
    warm intro
    Joel @ Beachwood
    Café · Piedmont Ave

    "Joel just took over Beachwood and is replacing every dish on the bar. He buys local and pays on time. Worth a coffee."

  4. 4

    It learns from what happens.

    A couple days later it checks in: "Did you connect? How'd it go?" Members reply in plain language. The agent turns that into a signal, updates their profile, and makes every future match sharper. This feedback loop is the core of the product — the network literally gets better the more it's used.

    Did you get a chance to talk with Joel?
    Yeah! He's ordering 30 mugs for trial. Loved his energy.
    Amazing. I'll keep an eye out for more cafés that fit that vibe.
    + wholesale match: confirmed + vibe: "warm, low-pressure" profile updated

§ 05 What we believe

Built on four quiet convictions.

i.

Effortless.

Join by text. No forms, no app, no "please sign up at our website." If your grandmother can text, she can join.

ii.

Proactive.

It introduces you. You don't have to search a directory or hope someone notices you exist.

iii.

Smarter over time.

Every connection teaches it. The product compounds — year two is when it really gets good.

iv.

Truly local.

Built around your neighborhood, your people, your events. Not "global community." This block.

Run a community? Let it run itself.

We're working with a small set of design partners — chambers, markets, collectives, and civic groups. If you've ever stayed up answering "do you know someone who…?", we'd love to talk.
